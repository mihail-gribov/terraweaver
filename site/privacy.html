Privacy

TerraWeaver is a personal memory that is meant to run on your own machine. This page is about the hosted demo at terraweaver.life, which is for evaluation only.

The hosted demo

  • Do not enter personal data. The demo is one shared world: whatever is said to it is visible to everyone who holds a key, and it is erased now and then.
  • Sign in with Google. We ask only for your email address and the account's identifier, to issue one access key per account. We keep the email, the identifier, the date the key was issued, and a one-way hash of the key. We do not keep the key itself, and we do not use your email for anything else.
  • What you say to the demo is kept in its journal and sent to the model providers below to be read and answered.
  • We do not sell or share data. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies. The sign-in uses a session cookie that lasts seven days.

What leaves the machine

On the demo and on your own machine alike, these are the only things that go out:

  • The text of what is said goes to NVIDIA Nemotron models on Nebius Token Factory, to be read, rewritten and decided on.
  • The text of journal entries goes to OpenRouter for the NVIDIA Nemotron embedding model, when TERRAWEAVER_EMBEDDINGS=openrouter is set, as on the demo. By default the embeddings are made locally and nothing goes out for them.
  • Web queries go to Tavily. They carry no person's name and no private detail.

Secrets are withheld before any model sees them. On the demo, everything typed is also in a shared journal that may be erased at any time.

Your own memory

When you run TerraWeaver yourself, the journal is a plain Markdown file on your machine and nothing is sent to us. Only the calls listed above go out.

Deleting your data
